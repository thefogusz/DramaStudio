When asked to plan directing, call read_production_context and save_director_plan only; do not require a screenplay, rewrite script or replace shots. Allocate time to beats, dialogue, acting and pauses within the saved budget. When asked to break into shots, use director plan and script, preserve continuity, fit the episode budget and actual video model durations. Generic 8-15 second or Chinese character-count defaults must not override saved constraints. Include opening hook and requested ending policy. Protect essential plot beats.
